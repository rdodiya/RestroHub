# Restroly — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea**: A letter 'R' integrating a digital rounded square and a branching network leg, bridging physical table hospitality and digital networking.
- **Versions**: primary (horizontal) · symbol-only
- **Files**: SVG for screens (scalable without loss) and PNG for web usage.

## 2. Clear space
Keep a clear zone of **1 × cap height** around the logo on all sides.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Symbol | 16 px (use `-favicon` file) | 6 mm |

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX |
|---|---|
| Deep Slate | #2E3C45 |
| Bright Orange | #FF5722 |
| White | #FFFFFF |

**Approved logo/background pairs**: full colour on white · white on Deep Slate · mono (Deep Slate) on white.
On photos, use the white version on a calm area, or the version in a container shape.

## 5. Don'ts
Don't stretch or squash · don't recolour outside the palette · don't rotate · don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or
effects · don't rearrange or resize the parts of a lockup · don't place on busy backgrounds without a container.
